"NGO" ek cheez nahi hai — teen layers hain

Ye wo baat hai jo Instagram pe koi theek se nahi bolta. Isse tum expert lagoge.

"31 lakh NGO" Layer 1 hai. "Videshi funding wale NGO project rok rahe hain" Layer 3 hai — jinke paas valid FCRA hai, wo sirf 16,301 the (July 2023). Ye do alag aabadiyan hain, lagbhag 190 guna ka farq. Report in dono ke beech phisalti rehti hai.

NGO Darpan registration itna kyun badha — asli wajah

Form CSR-1 ne CSR ka paisa lene ke liye Darpan ID ko lagbhag zaroori bana diya. Matlab curve ek kaagazi niyam ko dikha raha hai, kisi activism ke tsunami ko nahi. NGO badhe nahi — unhe register karne ko kaha gaya.

FCRA — aur abhi Parliament mein chal raha bill

  • 1976 — Emergency ke dauran bana, videshi paise ki chinta se.
  • 2010 — naya Act, purane ki jagah.
  • 2020 Amendment — sabse bada badlav:
    • Sub-granting band — ek FCRA wala doosre ko paisa nahi de sakta. Isne wo model khatam kar diya jisme ek badi NGO sau chhoti NGOs ko funding deti thi.
    • Admin kharcha 50% se ghatakar 20%.
    • Sara videshi paisa sirf SBI, New Delhi Main Branch mein.
    • Sab padadhikariyon ka Aadhaar zaroori.
  • April 2022 — Supreme Court ne ye sab barkarar rakha, Noel Harper v. Union of India: "videsh se paisa lena koi fundamental right nahi hai."

Ye abhi ki khabar hai — aur report mein nahi hai

FCRA (Amendment) Bill, 2026 — 25 March 2026 ko Lok Sabha mein introduce hua. Iske teen bade points:

  • Ek Designated Authority banega jo cancel ho chuki NGO ki sampatti apne kabze mein le sakta hai.
  • Renewal ke liye pichhle 2 saal mein kam se kam ₹10 lakh videshi contribution kharch kiya hona chahiye.
  • Certificate pe exact purpose aur exact states likhne honge.

Report kehti hai "Need of the hour: FCRA." Tum bol sakte ho — "wo bill abhi sansad mein hai." Yahi "abhi kyun" wala element hai, aur wahi sabse zyada share karata hai.

Duniya ke doosre desh kya karte hain

Ye tumhe unse aage le jaata hai — kyunki report ye comparison karti hi nahi.

DeshKanoonTareeka
USAFARA, 1938Sirf videshi paisa lena kaafi nahi. Tabhi lagta hai jab tum kisi videshi principal ke kehne ya control mein kaam kar rahe ho. Ye disclosure ka kanoon hai.

Ye farq yaad rakhna: FARA = batao tum kiske liye kaam kar rahe ho. FCRA = pehle ijazat lo. Dono transparency ke naam pe hain, par kaam alag karte hain. Ye ek line bol dogey toh log samajh jaayenge ki tumne padha hai.

Crackdown — numbers aur naam

Dono taraf ka sach

Ye dono ek saath sach hain. Dono pata hon toh koi comment tumhe ghera nahi sakta.

Sakhti ke haq mein
Zyadatar registered NGO hisaab file nahi karte — CBI ne khud SC ko bataya · Paisa ghumane ke asli case hain, Environics abhi court mein hai · Videshi paise se domestic policy prabhavit hona ek jaayaz chinta hai, har bada desh isko regulate karta hai · Supreme Court ne 2022 mein framework barkarar rakha
Zyadti ke khilaf
2020 ka sub-granting ban chhoti grassroots NGOs pe sabse zyada laga — unke paas apna FCRA nahi tha · Nuksaan seva dene walon ka hua, activists ka nahi · Enforcement chunindaa lagta hai — research institutions bahut hain list mein · Blanket niyam shell aur hospital mein farq nahi kar paate · Kai case mein courts ne rahat di hai

Northeast mein NGO zyada kyun hain — asli wajah

  • Sarkari machinery kamzor hai aur bhugol mushkil — isliye schemes jaanbujh kar NGOs ke through chalayi jaati hain
  • Church aur community networks society ke roop mein registered hain — wahan ye mainstream social infrastructure hai, koi kinare ka group nahi
  • Sixth Schedule ki autonomous councils aur paramparik sansthaein bhi society ban ke registered hoti hain
  • Manipur mein ~740 NGO per million — desh mein sabse zyada per-capita
  • Aur yahi India ke sabse kam industrialised rajya hain — isiliye startup kam hain

Project delay ke baare mein kya sach hai

2–3% GDP wala aankda kamzor hai. Par specific delays asli hain aur unke naam liye ja sakte hain:

  • POSCO — ₹52,000 crore ka Odisha steel project, 2017 mein chhod diya, ek dashak ke virodh ke baad
  • Vedanta / Niyamgiri — Supreme Court ne 2013 mein gram sabha ki manzoori zaroori ki; sabhaon ne mana kar diya
  • Kudankulam — saalon ka delay

"Project rukte hain aur uska paisa lagta hai" — ye bola ja sakta hai, naam ke saath. "NGO India ko 2–3% GDP ka nuksaan karte hain" — ye nahi. Yahi farq hai jahan koi tumhe test karega.

Pehla usool — narrative ke saath, kabhi khilaf nahi

Ye unka paid video hai. Agar reel unka apna data todegi toh approve kaun karega. Isliye humare paas teen hi moves hain:

CHHOD DO — claim galat hai aur narrative ke liye zaroori nahi · GHUM KAR NIKLO — point chahiye par seedha bolna risky · UPGRADE — unka number kamzor, humara mazboot, baat wahi.
DEBUNK kabhi nahi.

Causation ka sawaal — client jo chahta hai, aur jo safe hai

"NGO ki wajah se border states mein startup nahi aate" — ye fact ki tarah nahi bol sakte. Koi study nahi hai, aur counter turant aayega.

Par report khud causation claim karti hi nahi — wo sawaal poochhti hai: "Is India becoming a nation of builders or gatekeepers?" Toh hum bhi sawaal poochhenge. Data hum dikhayenge, natija darshak khud nikaalega. Sawaal ko koi debunk nahi kar sakta — aur ye unki report se zyada wafadar hai, kam nahi.
