# On-camera wording — NGO/startup reel

The deal: the agency's data and narrative go in the reel (that's what's being paid for).
My protection is **precision + attribution**, not censorship.

---

## Technique 1 — Say the precise thing, not the loose thing

This is the big one. It costs the narrative nothing and removes most of the debunk surface.

| ❌ Loose (indefensible) | ✅ Precise (same punch, now true) |
|---|---|
| "Har startup pe 2.5 NGOs" | "Har **DPIIT-registered** startup pe 2.5 NGOs" |
| "Sikkim mein 14 NGOs per startup" | "Sikkim mein **sirf 11 DPIIT-registered startups hain** — is liye ratio 14:1 dikhta hai" |
| "India mein 37 lakh NGOs" | "CBI ne Supreme Court ko bataya — **31 lakh se zyada registered societies**" |

Three extra words. The claim becomes literally accurate, and a debunker has nothing left
to correct. **This is the whole game.**

## Technique 2 — Attribute contested analysis out loud

Say the source *inside the sentence*. The burden moves to them.

| Claim | Exact wording |
|---|---|
| IB 2–3% GDP | *"2014 mein Intelligence Bureau ki ek report ne **claim kiya tha** ki foreign-funded NGO protests India ki GDP growth se 2 se 3 percent kha jaate hain."* |
| The ratio | *"Indian Matrix ki is report ke **mutabik**…"* |
| Environics Trust | *"Income Tax department ne Supreme Court mein **kaha hai** ki… — **maamla abhi court mein hai**."* |
| IB target sectors | *"Report ne coal, nuclear, ports aur dams ko specifically name kiya tha."* |

## Technique 3 — Lead with the one fact nobody can touch

*"2012 mein — Modi government se pehle — tab ke PM Manmohan Singh ne khud, Science magazine
ko diye interview mein, US-based NGOs ko Kudankulam ke protests ke liye blame kiya tha."*

Say flat. No hedge needed. And it pre-kills the "ye toh ek party ka agenda hai" reply.

## 🔴 Where attribution does NOT protect me

Three claims where "report ke hisaab se" is not a shield:

**1. Greenpeace lowering India's GDP** — In Indian defamation law, **republication is publication.**
Quoting someone else's defamatory statement about a named organisation makes me a publisher of it.
Greenpeace India has contested this and won FCRA rounds in the Delhi HC. Attribution ≠ immunity.

**2. The religious-conversion line** — Instagram's hate-speech policy does not care who I'm quoting.
Attribution unlocks nothing. Strike removes the reel, and possibly the account.

**3. "7% growth could be 10%"** — This is the subtle one. **It is not in the IB report.** It's the
brief's own arithmetic. So saying *"report ke hisaab se 10% ho sakta tha"* is **a false statement
about what the report says** — worse than asserting it myself, because it's checkable in one click.
If I want the line at all, it has to be: *"report ka argument hai ki…"* — and even then it's their
extrapolation, not a finding.

## The caveat that doubles as a re-hook

Best move in the reel. Around the 20–25s mark, right where retention dips:

> *"Lekin ek cheez ye numbers nahi batate — DPIIT startup ek **scheme** hai, jisme sirf 2 lakh
> companies registered hain. India mein actual registered businesses hain **7.83 crore**."*

Three things at once:
1. **Pattern interrupt** exactly where people scroll away
2. **Debunk-proofing** — the strongest attack is now already in my own reel, so a reaction video has nothing to add
3. **Credibility** — "ye banda dono side dikha raha hai" is why people share

Being the one who shows the caveat is a *positioning asset*, not a concession.
