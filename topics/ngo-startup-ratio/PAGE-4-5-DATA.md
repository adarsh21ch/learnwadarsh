### 🔴 Inki apni table hi inka argument tod deti hai

Dekho **"Active NGOs"** column ko:

> **Sikkim: 282 NGOs** → naksha DARK RED
> **Delhi: 36,032 NGOs** → naksha GREEN

Delhi mein Sikkim se **128 guna zyada** NGOs hain. Phir bhi Delhi hara hai aur Sikkim laal.

**Kyunki laal rang "zyada NGO" nahi dikhata — "kam startup" dikhata hai.**

Aur sabse bada example: **Lakshadweep — 17 NGOs, 3 startups.** Sattrah NGOs. Teen startups.
Ye "top 11 sensitive border areas" wali list mein hai. Ek aisi jagah jahan poore desh mein
sabse kam NGO hain (17), usko security threat bataया gaya hai — sirf isliye ki startup 3 hain.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki poori report bekaar hai. Iska matlab ye hai ki **naksha wala hissa
kamzor hai** — aur agar tum sirf naksha lekar chale, wahi tumhe pakde jaane ka sabse bada
risk hai.
