## The narrative they want
India has more NGOs than builders. Institutional energy has tilted toward "advocacy over
enterprise." The tilt is worst in border states, which makes it a national-security issue.
Conclusion: tighter FCRA enforcement.

Their framing line: *"Is India becoming a nation of builders or gatekeepers?"*
