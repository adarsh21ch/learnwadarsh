# Sponsored narrative — handling rules

**Hum kabhi client ki narrative ko contradict nahi karte.** Video unke liye ban rahi hai.
Agar reel unka apna data tod degi toh approve kaun karega.

Teen hi moves hain:

| Move | Kab use karo | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **CHHOD DO** | Claim galat/risky hai aur narrative ke liye zaroori nahi | 7%→10%, Greenpeace, conversion line, troop-road litigation |
| **GHUM KAR NIKLO** | Point chahiye par seedha bolna risky | "37 lakh" → *"report ka estimate 37 lakh hai"* |
| **UPGRADE** | Unka number kamzor, humara mazboot — same baat kehta hai | 37 lakh (no source) → CBI ka 31 lakh (Supreme Court affidavit) |

**Kabhi nahi: DEBUNK.** Unke naksha ya ratio ko video ke andar galat sabit karna = deal khatam.

---

## Causation ka rule

Client chahta hai: *"NGO ki wajah se border states mein startup nahi aate."*
Ye **fact ki tarah nahi bol sakte** — koi study nahi hai, aur counter turant aayega
(chhoti aabadi, koi industrial base nahi).

**Par report khud causation claim nahi karti — wo sawaal poochhti hai:**
*"Is India becoming a nation of builders or gatekeepers?"*

Toh hum bhi sawaal poochhenge. **Sawaal ko koi debunk nahi kar sakta.**

- ❌ "NGO ki wajah se wahan factory nahi lagti" — claim, proof chahiye
- ✅ "Sikkim mein 282 NGO hain. Aur 20 startups. Ye ratio kya keh raha hai?" — data + sawaal
- ✅ "Report ka sawaal yahi hai — builders ka desh ya gatekeepers ka?"

Data hum dikhate hain, natija darshak khud nikalta hai. Narrative same, risk zero.

## Safe wording — ratta maar lo

| Kehna hai | Aise bolo |
|---|---|
| Ratio | "Har ek **DPIIT-registered** startup pe 2.5 NGO" |
| 37 lakh | "**Report ka estimate** hai ki total 37 lakh ho sakte hain" |
| G20 | "**Absolute number** mein India sabse aage — G20 ka 29%" |
| IB GDP | "**IB ki 2014 report ne claim kiya tha** ki 2 se 3 percent" |
| Environics | "**Income Tax department ka claim hai** — maamla abhi court mein hai" |
| Accounts | "**CBI ne Supreme Court ko bataya** — 31 lakh se zyada societies" |

Har contested cheez ke saath source **bola** jaaye. Upar screen pe uska screenshot ho.
