# Hooks — NGO/Startup reel

**1 — Contradiction** ⭐ RECOMMENDED
> "Ye Modi government ne nahi kaha tha.
> 2012 mein Manmohan Singh ne kaha tha."
Kyun chalega: Sabse bada surprise. Pehle 3 second mein hi "ye toh propaganda hai" wala
reaction mar jaata hai — matlab wo aadhi audience bhi rukti hai jo warna scroll kar deti.

**2 — Shocking stat**
> "India mein 6 lakh 4 hazar NGO registered hain.
> Startups? 2 lakh 45 hazar."
Kyun chalega: Do numbers, koi bhaashan nahi. Report ka apna headline number.

**3 — Curiosity puzzle** ⭐ STRONG
> "Sikkim mein 282 NGO hain. Delhi mein 36,000.
> Toh naksha Sikkim ko laal aur Delhi ko hara kyun dikha raha hai?"
Kyun chalega: Asli paheli hai. Dimaag jawab dhoondhne lagta hai — aur jawab reel ke andar hai.

**4 — Question reframe** (sabse safe)
> "Sawaal ye nahi hai ki India mein NGO kitne hain.
> Sawaal ye hai ki unme se hisaab kitne dete hain."
Kyun chalega: Seedha bulletproof zameen pe le jaata hai — CBI ka data, koi debunk nahi.

**5 — Conditional warning**
> "Agar tumhe lagta hai NGO matlab sirf charity —
> ye reel end tak dekhna."
Kyun chalega: Purani identity ko challenge karta hai, isliye rukna padta hai.

**6 — Status gap / national**
> "Duniya ke har 3 NGO mein se 1 India mein hai.
> Aur factory lagane mein humein 2 saal lagte hain."
Kyun chalega: Sharam + gussa dono. ⚠️ Par per-capita wala risk hai — sirf tab jab
"absolute number" bola jaaye.

**7 — 🔀 Cross-niche transplant (FINANCE)**
> "Tumhare paas 10 bank account hon aur tum sirf 1 ka statement dekho —
> India ke NGO sector ke saath yahi ho raha hai."
Kyun chalega: Finance ka "tumhara paisa kahan ja raha hai" wala dar. Sabse universal
emotion hai, aur yahan seedha fit ho jaata hai.
