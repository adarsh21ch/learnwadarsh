# Angles — NGO-to-Startup ratio (Indian Matrix deal)

Format: **Split-screen talking head.** Face bottom half (selfie), visuals top half —
map, article SS, PIB pages, number pop-ups.

Format note: the top half carries the *source*, not just decoration. Saying "report ke
mutabik" while the actual page is on screen is the attribution shield made visible.

All three angles deliver the agency's payload (2.5:1 ratio, the map, border framing,
FCRA conclusion). They differ in what leads — which decides both reach and risk.

---

**Angle 1 — "Ye map dekhiye" (their frame, straight)**
Open on the map. Border states, 14:1, security framing. Closest to what the agency wants.
   → Why it hooks: the map is visually violent — dark red across the whole frontier. Security
     + identity emotion fires in under 2 seconds.
   → Debunk exposure: **HIGHEST.** Leading with the ratio means leading with the weakest claim.
     Survivable only with the precision fix + the mid-reel caveat, both non-optional.

**Angle 2 — "Ye Modi government ne nahi kaha tha. Manmohan Singh ne kaha tha."**
Open on the 2012 *Science* interview. A Congress PM blamed US NGOs first. Then widen to the
map and today's numbers.
   → Why it hooks: contradiction + it disarms "ye toh propaganda hai" in the first 3 seconds,
     so you keep the half of the audience that would otherwise swipe on sight. Bigger reach.
   → Debunk exposure: **LOWEST.** Opens on the single most verifiable fact in the deck.

**Angle 3 — "31 lakh NGOs hain. Kisi ko nahi pata kitne zinda hain."**
Open on the accountability black hole — CBI's Supreme Court affidavit, the filing rate. Then
the ratio and the map as evidence of the same vacuum.
   → Why it hooks: shock stat + an eerie curiosity gap ("nobody knows") that holds to the payoff.
   → Debunk exposure: **LOW.** Built on CBI-to-Supreme-Court, which nobody can touch.
